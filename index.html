Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Fibo 'n Achi ("the App") is a math practice app published by Jet Enterprise LLC. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores information only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers.

Information stored locally on your device includes:

  • Progress data (levels completed, stars earned, best times)
  • Settings (practice mode, auto-submit, haptic feedback, etc.)
  • Tip and tutorial preferences (e.g., "Do not show again" choices)
  • Zone unlock state for the map

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that collect personal information. We do not share any data with third parties.

Children

The App may be used by children. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not obtain parental consent for data collection. All data remains on the device.

Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by the App, uninstall the App from your device. You may also clear the App's data in your device settings (e.g., Settings → Apps → Fibo 'n Achi → Clear data on Android, or delete and reinstall on iOS).

Changes to This Policy

If we add features that collect or transmit data (such as leaderboards or progress reports), we will update this policy, indicate the date of the last update, and notify users of material changes through an in-app notice or app update notes.

Contact
